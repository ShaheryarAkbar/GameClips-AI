# GameClips AI – GTA 6 Scene Variety & Activity Expansion
**Master Production Directive & Content Rules**
*Saved on: 2026-11-26*
*Status: Permanent Active Directive for All Future Video Generations*

---

## Directive Overview
The current video production system is working well. It is successfully generating videos for all three categories:
1. Facts & Secrets
2. Cars & Comparisons
3. Stories & Lore

**Do not break, remove, redesign, or downgrade anything that is already working.** Keep the existing video quality, resolution, duration, editing, narration, subtitles, sound effects, transitions, research process, and daily production workflow unchanged.

The objective is to expand the variety of GTA 6 scenes and activities used in upcoming videos.

---

## 1. Stop Overusing Car-Driving Scenes
Currently, many generated videos mainly show cars driving. Car-related videos are fine, but the overall content should not repeatedly focus on driving.

GTA 6 has many different activities, locations, experiences, and gameplay possibilities. Future videos should explore this variety.

---

## 2. Expand the GTA 6 Scene Library
Include a wider range of scenes and activities, such as:

### Walking & Exploration
- Characters walking naturally through city streets.
- Exploring buildings, neighborhoods, and shopping areas.
- Walking through airports, hotels, beaches, and public places.
- Exploring forests, countryside, and remote locations.
- Visiting different areas and discovering interesting places.

### Vehicles & Transportation
- Driving cars through city streets and highways.
- Riding motorcycles and other vehicles.
- Driving through forests and countryside.
- Exploring airports and aircraft hangars.
- Stealing or taking an aircraft in a fictional GTA-style scenario, then taking off and flying it.
- Flying helicopters over the city, ocean, and other locations.
- Landing aircraft and continuing the story.

### Lifestyle & Activities
- Visiting parties, clubs, and entertainment locations.
- Going to hotels and checking into rooms.
- Spending time at beaches and swimming locations.
- Exploring restaurants, shops, and markets.
- Showing character interactions and everyday activities.
- Exploring luxury locations and different environments.

### Action & Crime Scenarios
- A fictional bank robbery storyline.
- Escaping from a robbery location.
- Visiting weapon shops and exploring available weapons.
- Using weapons in fictional gameplay scenarios.
- Police chases and confrontations.
- Escaping police through streets, countryside, or other locations.
- Helicopter or vehicle escape sequences.

*These are examples of possible content, not a mandatory checklist. Select scenes according to the topic and story.*

---

## 3. Create Complete, Connected Stories
Every video must have one central idea and a complete narrative.

### Examples of Storytelling Structure:
- **Airport Story:**
  1. Character arrives at the airport.
  2. Walks through the airport environment.
  3. Explores the aircraft area.
  4. Takes control of an aircraft in a fictional gameplay scenario.
  5. Starts the engine and takes off.
  6. Flies over the city.
  7. Ends with a suitable landing or story conclusion.

- **Bank Robbery Story:**
  1. Character arrives near a bank.
  2. Enters the location.
  3. The robbery begins.
  4. Character escapes from the bank.
  5. Police start chasing.
  6. A connected escape sequence follows.
  7. Story reaches a proper ending.

- **Beach Exploration Story:**
  1. Character drives or walks toward the beach.
  2. Arrives and explores the location.
  3. Walks along the beach.
  4. Interacts with the environment.
  5. Enjoys the location.
  6. Ends with a natural cinematic shot.

*Create original and interesting stories rather than repeating these exact sequences.*

---

## 4. Scene Selection Rules
- Select scenes based on the video's topic and script.
- Do not use car driving in every video.
- Explore different GTA 6 environments, activities, and experiences.
- Use multiple connected activities when they help complete the story.
- Maintain consistent characters, vehicles, locations, weather, lighting, and time of day.
- Every scene must logically connect to the previous scene.
- Prefer continuous cinematic sequences where practical.
- Do not randomly combine unrelated clips.
- Do not add scenes just to increase video duration.
- Keep the video engaging from beginning to end.

---

## 5. Daily Production: 3 Diverse Random Videos (Decoupled from Fixed Categories)
As per the production update on 2026-11-27, the rigid 3 fixed categories (`Facts & Secrets`, `Cars & Comparisons`, `Stories & Lore`) have been completely removed to avoid forcing repetitive topics or car-driving overuse.

Instead, every daily run autonomously generates **exactly 3 distinct, high-variety videos** selected randomly across the broad GTA 6 scene library:
1. **Walking & Exploration** (e.g., City streets, neighborhoods, shopping districts, luxury plazas, terminal exploration, beaches on foot)
2. **Vehicles & Transportation / Aviation** (e.g., Aircraft boarding & runway takeoff, helicopter flight, off-road expeditions, superbikes, boats/yachts)
3. **Lifestyle & Entertainment** (e.g., Nightclubs, beach crowds, rooftop pools, festivals, restaurants, social interactions)
4. **Action & Crime Scenarios** (e.g., Bank heists, convenience store robberies, tactical shootouts, high-stakes police evasion)

Each video must be 100% focused on its own central activity and story, with zero repetitive car driving and zero asset duplication across daily runs.

---

## 6. Visual Quality & Authenticity
Maintain the existing visual quality and production standards.
- Use authorized real GTA gameplay only when the required usage rights are documented.
- Otherwise, use original fictional realistic game-style visuals and clearly label them as fictional.
- Never present fictional generated scenes as actual GTA 6 gameplay.
- Do not use unauthorized YouTube or TikTok clips.
- Do not replace proper video scenes with static images, slideshows, or unrelated stock footage.
- Maintain professional narration, synchronized subtitles, sound effects, ambience, and natural transitions.

---

## 7. Operational Enforcement
This is an expansion of the existing scene and activity library, not a replacement of the current production system.

Keep everything that is already working. Start incorporating more diverse GTA 6-style activities and environments in future video generations.

The goal is to make the channel feel like it covers the complete GTA 6 experience — exploration, walking, vehicles, flying, lifestyle, locations, adventures, action, and storytelling — instead of showing mostly cars driving in every video.
